European IT LMS · UI guide v2

Friendly, calm and clear: how the panels look, move and behave

European IT's colours and type, with the layout habits of the School of GenZ LMS: what to do next comes first, icons do the talking, and motion only shows that something happened.

01 · Principles

Six rules every screen follows

Next step firstEvery dashboard opens with what to do now: continue watching, the class starting soon, or what needs your attention.
One cyan button per screenThe main action is the only cyan button. Everything else is a ghost button or a text link.
Icons live in tilesNav items, lists and stats get a tinted tile. Its colour tells you the kind of thing before you read it.
Sticker energy only where you can clickTiles tilt and cards lift only when you can act on them. Floating stickers appear only in welcome banners.
Status is never colour aloneEvery badge carries a word: Approved, Due in 5 days, Overdue, Locked. It still works in black and white.
Phone firstBottom tabs, one column, 44px taps and short labels, because most students study on budget Android phones.

04 · Motion

Quick, quiet, and only when something happens

Moment Where How it moves Timing
Page rise Every section on load Fades up 14px, one after another 520ms · 60ms apart
Card lift Clickable cards Rises 3px, soft shadow, cyan edge 200ms
Tile tilt Nav icons, list and card tiles Turns 8° and grows 8%, springs back 200ms spring
Bar fill Progress bars Grows from the left 1.1s, 250ms delay
Ring draw Course progress, quiz score Draws round to the value 1.4s
Count up Stat numbers Counts from 0 to the value once 1.4s
Bell ring Notification button Swings on hover; unread dot pulses 700ms · 2.4s loop
Dropdown pop Notifications panel Scales in from the bell 260ms spring
Live pulse Classes starting soon, running batches Green dot ripples 2s loop
Success check Payment done, password changed Circle pops, tick draws in 500ms + 700ms
With "reduce motion" turned on in the phone or computer settings, everything appears in its final state with no movement.
05 · Phones

The sidebar becomes a bottom tab bar

Same pattern as the School of GenZ LMS. Below 720px the sidebar moves to the bottom of the screen with icon-over-label tabs, cards stack in one column, and every tap target stays at least 44px. It is already built into the shared stylesheet, so every panel screen gets it.
