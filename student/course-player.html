Colour and contrast for social posts

18 min · Mentor: Nadia Rahman · Last watched today
Lesson 8 opens when you finish this video
6:24 left to watch
Previous lessonLocked until this video is watched to the end

Colour and contrast decide whether a social post gets read. See how to pick a palette that fits the brand, check that the text stays readable on a phone screen, and keep the busiest part of the image away from the caption.

In this lesson
  • Build a palette of one main colour, one accent and two neutrals for a brand
  • Check text against its background: 4.5:1 for small text, 3:1 for large
  • Place captions and logos on calm areas of a photo, or add a soft overlay
